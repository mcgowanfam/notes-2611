Piccadilly line westbound → Heathrow Terminals 2 & 3
Westbound (Platform 5) toward Heathrow. One seat, no changes. TfL timetable: about 57 min King's Cross → Heathrow Terminals 2 & 3 on trains to Heathrow T123 + 5 (Terminal 5); around 6:30–7:00 these leave roughly every 9–12 min (e.g. 6:34, 6:43, 6:55 Mon–Thu).

Don't board trains ending at Northfields, Rayners Lane or Uxbridge: they don't go to Heathrow (the line splits at Acton Town). Check the platform board or the front of the train.
Pay: each person taps their own contactless card or phone, and uses the same card to tap in and out. Before 9:30 a.m. is a peak fare; the TfL fare finder showed £5.90 per person for central London ↔ Heathrow on the Piccadilly line (checked Oct 2026 for Euston; same zones).
Bags: the Underground fills up toward rush hour, and bags take space; stand clear of the doors. Step-free with lifts at both ends: King's Cross St Pancras has lifts to the Piccadilly platforms (a boarding ramp is available from staff), and Heathrow Terminals 2 & 3 has lifts with level-access humps on the platforms (TfL step-free guide).
